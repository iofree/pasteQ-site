## Take a saved reply from Mac to iPhone

1. **Organize on Mac.** Copy a reply you use often and add it to Pin or a custom group in PasteQ.
2. **Check it on iPhone.** Enable iCloud sync on both devices, then open PasteQ on your phone and confirm the text has arrived.
3. **Reuse it while typing.** Add and switch to the PasteQ keyboard, tap the saved text, and review it before sending.

## Before you download

### How is PasteQ different from Universal Clipboard?

Apple's Universal Clipboard helps you transfer something you just copied to another device. PasteQ focuses on searching history, filtering by source and organizing reusable content so you can find saved items later. [Learn about multi-device sync](/en/guide/multi-device-sync).

### Does iPhone record everything I copy in the background?

No. Clipboard recording on Mac and the iPhone workflow are different. The iOS app should not be treated as a continuous background recorder for every app. On your phone, you can find synced records and reuse saved text through the keyboard.

### Is my data local or in iCloud?

PasteQ stores data on your device. When you enable iCloud sync, data also syncs through your personal iCloud account. Sync needs a network connection and the relevant system settings. See the [privacy policy](/en/guide/privacy) for the terms.

Most features are free. Some advanced features are paid.
