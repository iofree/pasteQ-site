## Keep your clipboard workflow on the keyboard

Press **Option + C** on Mac to open your clipboard, then search for copied text, images and links. Use source filters, Pin and groups to find saved items. Open, search, filter, copy and paste with shortcuts; the hint bar shows which actions are available as you work.

[Get started with clipboard history →](/en/guide/getting-started)

## Take a saved reply from Mac to iPhone

1. **Organize on Mac.** Copy a reply you use often and add it to Pin or a custom group in PasteQ.
2. **Check it on iPhone.** Enable iCloud sync on both devices, then open PasteQ on your phone and confirm the text has arrived.
3. **Reuse it while typing.** Add and switch to the PasteQ keyboard, tap the saved text, and review it before sending.

## Copy text. Back Tap. Reuse on iPhone. {#back-tap}

An outline for your next draft, a short bio for a new post, or a portfolio link for a collaboration reply. Set up the PasteQ shortcut and keyboard, copy text or a link, then trigger Back Tap to capture that item. Open the PasteQ keyboard and pick items from Pinned to insert them one by one, without opening the main app first.

<FeatureShowcase type="back-tap" />

Quick capture requires iOS 16+. Back Tap needs a compatible iPhone; on iPadOS 16+, run the shortcut directly. The keyboard works in fields that support third-party keyboards.

## Before you download

### Does the app launcher work on iPhone?

The app launcher is Mac-only. On iPhone and iPad, PasteQ offers clipboard management, iCloud sync and a keyboard for saved text. Mobile quick capture requires you to run a shortcut. [Explore the Mac app launcher](/en/guide/getting-started#app-launcher).

### How is PasteQ different from Universal Clipboard?

Apple's Universal Clipboard helps you transfer something you just copied to another device. PasteQ focuses on searching history, filtering by source and organizing reusable content so you can find saved items later. [Learn about multi-device sync](/en/guide/multi-device-sync).

### Does iPhone record everything I copy in the background?

No. On iPhone, each text item or link is captured when you run the shortcut or trigger Back Tap. Copying alone does not start continuous background recording. Keep automatic pinning enabled (the default) to reuse captured items from Pinned in the PasteQ keyboard. [See setup and requirements](/en/guide/back-tap-clipboard).

### Is my data local or in iCloud?

PasteQ stores data on your device. When you enable iCloud sync, data also syncs through your personal iCloud account. Sync needs a network connection and the relevant system settings. See the [privacy policy](/en/guide/privacy) for the terms.

Most features are free. Some advanced features are paid.
